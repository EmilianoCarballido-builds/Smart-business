# DECISIONS — Week 5

- Chose the navigation vacuum, not new diagnostic accuracy.
- Built around a fictional existing abnormal retinal screening result.
- Chose a no-database prototype to minimize privacy/security risk.
- Used structured handoff state as the Week 5 signal.
- Simulated AI output is visibly labeled rather than pretending a live medical AI is running.
- Automation stops after two unsuccessful attempts.
- Human navigator acceptance does not equal resolution.
- Clinical diagnosis, urgency and treatment remain outside the product.
